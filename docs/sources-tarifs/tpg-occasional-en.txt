Tickets and day passes
Do you occasionally travel using public transport? No need for a travel pass: tickets and day passes are made for you!
With a ticket or a day pass, you can travel hassle-free on our entire network.
You travel  in Geneva only
You can choose between several Tout Genève tickets and day passes.
Tout Genève Zone 10 Tickets
Tout Genève Zone 10 Day Passes
Zone 10, All of Geneva, encompasses the entire canton of Geneva. There are two types of tickets valid in Tout Genève Zone 10 only:
Short hop:  valid for a 3-stop journey (one way) on a tpg line or a crossing with the Mouettes Genevoises. This ticket is not valid on board SBB/SNCF trains and Alpbus coaches.
Tout Genève ticket: valid for 60 minutes
There are two day passes for travel within Geneva (Zone 10).
Day pass: valid from the time of purchase until 5:00 a.m. the next day
Day pass from 9:00 a.m.: valid from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m. the next day
The full-fare Tout Genève day passes are valid for two people traveling together on Saturday or Sunday. This advantage does not apply to day passes valid from 9:00 a.m., which are for individual use.
Find out the rates of tickets and day passes
Are you traveling  between Geneva and France?
To travel between Geneva and the following French regions, you need a ticket or a Léman Pass card:
Veigy (zone 200),
Annemasse (zone 210),
St-Julien – including Collonges and Valleiry (Zone 230),
Pougny (zone 240),
Pays-de-Gex (zone 250),
Saint-Gervais-les-Bains-Le-Fayet, Thonon-Evian (zone 300),
Annecy-Pringy-Groisy (zone 380)
Valserhône (zone 400)
Léman Pass Tickets
Léman Pass Day Passes
There are several types:
Léman Pass ticket: valid for one trip, one trip-zone route or one zone-trip-zone route: 180 minutes.
Léman Pass Multizones ticket: this allows you to travel from Zone 10 to an adjacent French zone and vice versa.
Valid 90 minutes.
Cross-border short hop: this allows you to make two stops on either side of the border without returns or connections.
It is valid only on TPG lines for a one-way journey without return or transfer for 30 minutes. Not valid on board Alpbus trains and coaches.
Léman Pass animal ticket: valid for pets according to the vehicle operator's conditions.
There are two types:
1-day Léman Pass card: valid all day from the time of purchase until the end of the service.
5-day Léman Pass multizones card: valid from the date of purchase for 5 days in Zone 10 and an adjacent French zone.
Supplementary Léman Pass ticket
If you have a ticket, a day pass, or an unireso pass and wish to travel to a neighboring French zone or vice versa, all you need is a supplementary Léman Pass ticket. It is valid for 90 minutes in the additional zone acquired.
For trips to France, you can buy it with our tpg app, at one of our distributors, or at one of our agencies.
Its price varies according to the supplementary zone where you wish to go:
Zones 240 or 250: € 1.60/CHF 1.60,
Zones 200 or 230: € 1.60/CHF 1.60,
Zones 210:  €1.70 / CHF 1.60,
Zone 10:  CHF 3.00/€ 3.20
Are you traveling  in France only?
You can buy your local tickets for travel in the regions of Veigy (Zone 200), St-Julien including Collonges and Valleiry (Zone 230), Pougny (Zone 240), and Pays-de-Gex (Zone 250) from our resellers in France and from the on-board distributors of our cross-border routes.
Tickets valid in the Annemasse area (Zone 210) are distributed by the TAC .
Are you traveling throughout Switzerland?
To travel throughout Switzerland from Geneva, choose the national day pass . With it, you can travel freely throughout Switzerland, including the unireso zone, and as far as Annemasse station with the Léman Express.
tpgPay, your reloadable card for buying tickets at distributors
The reloadable tpgPay card allows you to buy your ticket or day pass quickly and easily from the distributors on the network or on board our vehicles.
Secure, ultra-fast, proactive and eco-friendly, it offers you many advantages to continue travelling on our public transport network! Visit tpg.shop now to secure and optimally manage your tpg pay card.
It is on sale in our tpg agencies and at authorized dealers in Switzerland and France. Two types of cards are available: CHF or Euro. They are reloadable for amounts of 20/50/100 CHF or €. Cards are valid for five years from the date of activation.
Manage and secure your TPG Pay card on  tpg.shop
To ensure the security of your tpg pay card and benefit from our state-of-the-art management tools, register your card now at  tpg.shop  . You can now benefit from the following advantages:
Block your TPG Pay card in case of loss or theft
Check your card balance at any time
Set your card threshold and be alerted by e-mail or SMS when your balance is reached
Be alerted by e-mail or text message when your card expires
But also:
Buy tickets from ticket machines in seconds with contactless payment
Reload your card according to the desired amount: 20 / 50 / 100 / CHF or € in tpg agencies or at our retailers.

tpg pay  , instructions for use
Buy your ticket or day pass with tpg pay
Buying your ticket with the tpg pay card is easy:
Choose your ticket
Place your tpg pay card on the chip reader
Get your ticket in seconds!
Check your tpg pay balance
You can check the remaining credit on your tpg pay card at any time. Simply press the tpg pay button on the screen of one of our dispensers and place your card on the contactless reader of the machine. Your card balance will appear on the screen.
