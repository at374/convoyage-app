# A79: conditional published passage prices

`data/tolls/reviewed/aliae-a79-2026.json` retains all ten products from Annex XII of the 28 January 2026 tariff order, effective 1 February 2026: 60 light-vehicle amounts (classes 1/2/5, categories e/ne) and 80 heavy-vehicle amounts (classes 3/4, four native Euro groups). These 140 source amounts are not 140 physical tolls or complete route prices.

Primary tariff source: https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/eli/arrete/2026/1/28/TRAT2534086A/jo/article_snum13 . The official HTML tables were reviewed. Local retrieval of the operator's 2026 PDF returned HTTP 403; there is no archived-byte/hash verification claim. Native category codes are preserved; an electric/fuel label, badge or vehicle class alone does not establish tariff eligibility.

The operator's current explanation identifies three chargeable sections, each surrounding an interchange: Le Montet, Montbeugny and Molinet. The movement is the published `Ouest`, `Transit` or `Est` product; it is not simply the vehicle's compass bearing. Transit is quoted once, not calculated by adding its two half-passages. Deux-Chaises/Ouest is also present in the tariff table but does not price the upstream A71 closed-system trip.

Operator structure: https://paiement.aliae.com/fr/quelles-seront-les-sections-a-peage-en-flux-libre-payantes-et-les-sections-avec-gratuite-de-circulation-sur-l-a79--6430886160372004313

`toll-a79-prices.cjs` exposes `quotePassage({date, asOf, point, movement, vehicleClass, nativeEmissionCategory})` for classes 1/2/5. Classes 3/4 instead require `nativeEuroGroup` (0-3, 4, 5 or 6); the two contexts cannot be combined. Wrong/guessed identifiers, missing vehicle categories, dates outside the reviewed tariff period, future source checks and checks older than seven days refuse a price. The lookup does not discover the road route or verify vehicle eligibility.

`subtotalKnownPassages({...context, passages:[{point,movement},...]})` sums only caller-specified products. A repeated pricing point, whether duplicated or assigned competing movements, rejects the subtotal. A passage cannot override the shared date or vehicle context. Empty or partly unknown input never becomes a zero or partial quote. The result explicitly has `amount:null`, `completeRoutePrice:null` and `routeVerified:false`; only `subtotal` contains the source-product sum. For example, the three Transit products for class 1/e total 4.40 EUR, versus 3.40 EUR for class 1/ne. This is not an end-to-end quote including Deux-Chaises or the A71.

## Geography remains unresolved

The ART sector declaration (situation on 25 February 2025) supplies directional gantry identifiers and kilometre references. It does not supply verified GPS lane positions, and its western Montbeugny kilometre reference differs by direction. No geographic interpolation or guessed equality between opposite-direction gates is imported.

Historical sector source: https://www.autorite-transports.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/declaration-secteur-set_aliae_20250506.pdf . The concession conditions also distinguish low-emission/light-vehicle modulation from heavy-vehicle Euro modulation: https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000041722783 . Sector evidence is not a current road access/traffic certificate.
